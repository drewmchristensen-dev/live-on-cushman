Fairbanks Apartments

Remodeled studios on Cushman

1010 Cushman St and 1002 Cushman St, Fairbanks, AK 99701

Twenty-eight apartments in two buildings side by side on Cushman, being remodeled one unit at a time: new floors, new kitchens, new baths. Rent covers your utilities, which matters at forty below. Cats welcome. A laundry room in each building.

You are on the downtown grid: the transit center at 5th and Cushman, the Chena riverfront, coffee and restaurants within a few blocks, and the Fort Wainwright main gate a few minutes' drive east.

The Nest

Furnished, upstairs from 4th Avenue, since 1930

400 Cushman St, residential entrance at 609½ 4th Ave, Fairbanks, AK 99701

The Nest has stood at the corner of 4th and Cushman since 1930, with two storefronts at street level and sixteen furnished apartments above. Studios and one-bedrooms come furnished with Wi-Fi included, so a travel contract or a rotation starts the day you land. The coin laundry room is in the building. Live here and you are part of a century of downtown Fairbanks.

Next year the building becomes the landmark on the block: new exteriors, and a mural across the upper floors painted by a commissioned artist. Residents will watch it go up from their windows.
